Frequently asked questions

How long does credit repair actually take?

Most clients begin seeing changes within two weeks of their first dispute round. Full case resolution typically wraps up within 90 days, depending on how many items are being addressed.

How much does this cost?

Pricing is fully custom — it depends on your credit report and how much work is involved. Call for a free consultation and we'll walk you through a straightforward quote before you commit to anything.

Is credit repair legitimate?

Yes. Disputing inaccurate, outdated, or unverifiable items on your credit report is a right afforded to every consumer. We handle that process for you, correctly and transparently.
